# Pitch Atlas Privacy Policy (iOS)

_Last updated: 2026-11-05. Candidate build 1.2.0 (14); publication at the live privacy URL requires separate verification._

Pitch Atlas is a native reference and community app. The bundled reference manual works without an account. Community actions use a contributor record, which can be created anonymously when you first submit content, report, block, or contact the developer. Apple or email sign-in is optional unless you want to claim that record across devices or upload a still image.

## Data we collect

Depending on the features you use, Pitch Atlas collects:

- Account email address and a contributor ID, including anonymous IDs.
- Field notes, discussion posts, replies, public-post suggestions, reports, and block records.
- Still images you select for upload through PhotosPicker.
- Messages you submit through Contact developer and an optional reply email address, linked to your contributor ID when submitted.

Opening Contact developer does not submit data. The form sends your message only when you choose Send to developer.

Pitch Atlas does not collect live camera footage, native video uploads, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## How we use data

We use this information to maintain your session, provide community features, review and publish permitted content, handle reports and blocks, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and process account or privacy requests.

Published community posts and approved notes or images can be visible to other readers. Reports, block records, and messages to the developer are private. Your optional support reply email is not published with community content.

We do not sell data or track users across apps or websites. Collected data is used for app functionality, customer support, and community safety.

## Local preferences and unsent reports

The app stores your versioned terms and 18+ confirmation, hidden-content preferences, and unsent reports on your device. Hide takes effect immediately, including offline. Unsent reports remain available for retry in Account > Safety until the server accepts them. Local preferences do not themselves submit a moderation report.

## Photos and teaching clips

The iOS app accepts still images only and re-encodes selected images before upload when feasible to reduce metadata exposure. Image uploads require a claimed account and acceptance of the image terms. Uploaded images remain private until approved. Do not upload content you do not own, images of minors, abusive or unsafe content, or medical-advice claims.

Optional teaching clips use TikTok's official player after you tap their poster. The player connects to TikTok, and its use is governed by TikTok's privacy practices. Pitch Atlas does not download or rehost those clips.

## Account deletion and support retention

Use Delete account in Account to remove an anonymous or claimed account. The backend removes the account and its account-linked community records and owned discussion-media objects.

Deleting an account removes the contributor-ID link from messages previously sent to the developer. The message text and optional reply email remain in the private support inbox for customer support and safety. There is no automatic retention deadline or scheduled deletion for those messages. To request access to or deletion of support information, use Contact developer in Account > Safety or Support.

## Age requirement

Posting, replying, filing field notes, suggesting public posts, and uploading require acceptance of the Community Terms and EULA and confirmation that you are 18 or older. Reporting, blocking, and contacting the developer remain available without contribution consent. The reference manual remains available without an account.

## Contact

Use Contact developer in Account > Safety or Support to send a private message to Austin Humphrey. A reply email is optional; provide one if you want an email response.
